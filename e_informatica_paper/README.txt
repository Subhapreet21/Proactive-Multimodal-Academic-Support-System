Dear Authors,

We are excited to announce the release of our new LaTeX template for 
submissions to the e-Informatica Software Engineering Journal. This 
updated template is designed to streamline the publication process 
and ensure a consistent, high-quality presentation of your research.

In this ZIP file, you will find the following:

- README.txt - this file

- template_tutorial.dist.pdf - compiled sample paper with short tutorial
  presenting template
  
- template_tutorial.tex, template_tutorial.bib - LaTeX sources of 
  template_tutorial.dist.pdf with code samples demonstrating how 
  to use our template

- eisej_paper.tex - main paper file to fill up with your research results
- eisej_paper.bib - bibliography file

- other files required to build the paper


We kindly ask that you use this template when preparing your paper 
for submission. 

Please DO NOT modify the template layout (fonts, columns, etc.), and 
only INCLUDE LaTeX packages NECCESARY to present your content.

One area, we would like to emphasize, is the importance of a properly 
formatted bibliography. The editorial office strongly emphasizes 
maintaining the high quality of our journal, and a significant 
portion of the publishing time is often devoted to resolving issues 
with the bibliography. By ensuring your references are correctly 
formatted according to the template, you can help streamline 
the publication process and publish your paper faster.

If you encounter any problems or have questions while using it, 
please don't hesitate to contact us at: e-informatica@pwr.edu.pl. 

Our team is here to support you throughout the submission and publication 
process.

We look forward to receiving your high-quality submissions and working 
together to advance the field of software engineering.

Best regards,
e-Informatica Software Engineering Journal
